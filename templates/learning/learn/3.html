Company Basics & Sector Analysis

Not all companies are equal. Learn how to tell a strong company from a weak one, and why knowing the sector is half the battle on NEPSE.

Lesson 3 of 560%

Simple Explanation

Imagine a shopping mall. There's a food court, a clothing section, an electronics zone. Each zone behaves differently — food is always busy, electronics peak during festivals.

NEPSE is like that mall. It has sectors — groups of similar companies. And just like zones in a mall, different sectors behave differently.

Within each sector, you need to check 3 things to know if a company is healthy:

  • Profit — The money a company keeps after paying all expenses. No profit = no future.
  • Dividend — Two types: Cash Dividend and Bonus Share.
  • Growth — Profit increasing every year is attractive.

Simple rule: Profit tells you alive. Dividend generous. Growth future. You want all three.

सरल व्याख्या

शपिङ मलको कुरा गरौं। हरेक जोनको व्यवहार फरक हुन्छ। NEPSE पनि त्यस्तै मल हो। यसमा Sector (समूह) हरू छन्।

प्रत्येक Sector भित्र ३ वटा कुरा जाँच्नुपर्छ:

  • Profit (नाफा) — कम्पनीले राख्ने पैसा।
  • Dividend (लाभांश) — Cash Dividend र Bonus Share।
  • Growth (वृद्धि) — नाफा बढ्दै छ भने आकर्षक।

सरल नियम: Profit = जीवित, Dividend = उदार, Growth = भविष्य। तीनवटै चाहिन्छ।
