Making Buy, Hold & Sell Decisions

Everything you've learned comes down to 3 decisions. This lesson gives you a simple framework to decide when to enter, when to wait, and when to exit — without relying on tips or emotions.

Lesson 5 of 5 100%

Simple Explanation

Think of investing like catching a bus. You wait at the right stop (buy), you enjoy the ride to your destination (hold), and you get off when you arrive or if the bus breaks down (sell).

Most beginners do the opposite — they run after a bus that already left (buy at peak), jump off at every bump (sell in panic), or fall asleep and miss their stop (hold forever without a reason).

Here's the simple checklist for each decision:

The golden rule: Never buy on tips. Never sell in panic. Always have a reason.

Practical Example

Let's follow Ram, a beginner investor, with NMB Bank:

  • Buy at Rs. 380 — Strong profits, sector trending, high volume.
  • Hold at Rs. 420 — Normal fluctuation, still profitable.
  • Sell at Rs. 510 — Target profit + NRB tight policy.

Emotion-free investing: checklist wins.

Why It Matters

  • FOMO is real — Checklist stops you from buying at the peak.
  • Panic selling destroys wealth — Check fundamentals first.
  • Knowing when to sell is hardest — Have profit target & stop-loss.
  • Paper trading builds confidence before real money.
