Reading Prices, Charts & the Numbers That Matter

The stock market speaks in numbers. This lesson teaches you how to read them — what each number means, what to ignore, and what actually signals an opportunity.

Lesson 2 of 540%

Simple Explanation

When you open the NEPSE site or your broker app, you see a flood of numbers. Most beginners get overwhelmed. But you only need to understand 4 key numbers to start:

  • Price — The cost of 1 share right now. Like the price tag on a product. If Nabil Bank shows Rs. 480, you pay Rs. 480 for 1 share.
  • Volume — How many shares were bought + sold today. High volume = lots of interest. Low volume = few people care.
  • Turnover — Total rupees exchanged today. Volume x Average Price = Turnover. It tells you how much actual money moved.
  • NEPSE Index — A single number that shows the overall market direction. Going up = most stocks rising. Going down = most stocks falling.

Think of it like a hospital: Price is the patient's temperature. Volume is how many visitors came. Turnover is the total bill. Index is the hospital's overall health score.

The golden rule: Price moving UP with HIGH volume = genuine strength. Price moving UP with LOW volume = suspicious, might reverse. Always check volume alongside price.

Why It Matters

  • Price without volume is misleading — A stock can go up 5% on just 10 shares traded. That's not real demand. That's an illusion.
  • Volume confirms the move — Price going up + volume going up = real buyers stepping in. This is trustworthy.
  • Turnover tells you the money size — 10,000 shares of NTC at Rs. 900 = Rs. 90 lakh turnover. 10,000 shares of a Rs. 50 micro-finance = Rs. 5 lakh. Same volume, very different money.
  • Index gives you the big picture — If NEPSE Index is down 50 points but your stock is up, your stock is swimming against the current. Be cautious.

Quick Action Insight

  • Pick any 3 stocks — For each, find: today's Open, Close, High, Low, Volume, and Turnover. Write them down.
  • Check the Index — Note the NEPSE Index value. Is it up or down from yesterday? By how many points?
  • Find the volume-price combo — Look for one stock that went UP with HIGH volume. And one that went UP with LOW volume. Notice the difference.
  • Check "Top Turnover" — The NEPSE homepage shows stocks with highest turnover. These are where the big money is flowing today. Just observe the names.

Still no investing! You're learning to read the dashboard before driving the car. One more lesson to go before you start analyzing companies.

Mini Recap

  • Price = cost of 1 share; Open/Close/High/Low show the day's range
  • Volume = number of shares traded; high volume confirms the price move is real
  • Turnover = total money traded; reveals where big money is flowing
  • NEPSE Index = overall market temperature; up = most stocks rising, down = most falling
  • Always read Price + Volume together — price alone tells half the story

सरल व्याख्या

जब तपाईंं NEPSE साइट वा Broker को एप खोल्नुहुन्छ, तपाईंंले संख्याको बाढी देख्नुहुन्छ। सुरुवातकर्ताहरू अलमलिन सक्छन्। तर सुरु गर्न ४ वटा मुख्य संख्या मात्र बुझ्नुपर्छ:

  • Price (मूल्य) — अहिले १ वटा शेयरको भाउ।
  • Volume (भोल्युम) — आज कति वटा शेयर किनबेच भयो।
  • Turnover (टर्नओभर) — आज कुल कति रुपैयाँको कारोबार भयो।
  • NEPSE Index (सूचकांक) — एउटा नम्बर जसले समग्र बजारको दिशा देखाउँछ।

अस्पतालको उदाहरणले बुझ्नुहोस्: Price भनेको बिरामीको ज्वरो हो। Volume भनेको कति आगन्तुक आए भन्ने हो। Turnover भनेको कुल बिल हो। Index भनेको अस्पतालको समग्र स्वास्थ्य स्कोर हो।

सुनौलो नियम: Price बढ्दैछ + HIGH Volume = वास्तविक शक्ति। Price बढ्दैछ + LOW Volume = शंकास्पद, उल्टिन सक्छ। सधैं Price सँगै Volume जाँच्नुहोस्।

यो किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ

  • Volume विनाको Price भ्रामक हुन्छ — एउटा स्टक केवल १० शेयर कारोबार भएर ५% बढ्न सक्छ। त्यो वास्तविक माग होइन।
  • Volume ले चाल पुष्टि गर्छ — Price बढ्दैछ + Volume बढ्दैछ = वास्तविक किन्नेहरू आएका छन्।
  • Turnover ले पैसाको आकार बताउँछ — NTC को १०,००० शेयर Rs. ९०० मा = Rs. ९० लाख Turnover। Rs. ५० को माइक्रोफाइनान्सको १०,००० शेयर = Rs. ५ लाख।
  • Index ले ठूलो तस्वीर दिन्छ — यदि NEPSE Index ५० अंक घटिरहेको छ तर तपाईंंको स्टक बढिरहेको छ भने, सावधान हुनुहोस्।
अवस्थायसको अर्थके गर्ने

द्रुत कार्य अन्तर्दृष्टि

  • जुनसुकै ३ वटा स्टक छान्नुहोस् — प्रत्येकको लागि: आजको Open, Close, High, Low, Volume, र Turnover खोज्नुहोस्।
  • Index जाँच्नुहोस् — NEPSE Index को मान नोट गर्नुहोस्।
  • Volume-Price को मिश्रण खोज्नुहोस् — उच्च Volume संग बढेको र कम Volume संग बढेको फरक हेर्नुहोस्।
  • "Top Turnover" हेर्नुहोस् — ठूलो पैसा कहाँ गइरहेको छ हेर्नुहोस्।

अहिले पनि लगानी गर्ने होइन! तपाईंं गाडी चलाउनु अघि ड्यासबोर्ड पढ्न सिक्दै हुनुहुन्छ।

संक्षिप्त सारांश

  • Price = १ शेयरको भाउ; Open/Close/High/Low ले दिनभरको दायरा देखाउँछ
  • Volume = कारोबार भएको शेयरको संख्या; High Volume ले Price चाल वास्तविक हो भन्ने पुष्टि गर्छ
  • Turnover = कारोबार भएको कुल रकम
  • NEPSE Index = समग्र बजारको तापमान
  • सधैं Price + Volume सँगै पढ्नुहोस्
